Privacy Policy - Clip Factory

Last updated: 3 October 2026

What data the app uses

Clip Factory signs in with Google only to upload videos to the user's own YouTube channel and to read that channel's basic information (name, id, upload limits). It uses the scopes youtube.upload and youtube.readonly.

How data is stored

Sign-in tokens are stored only on the user's own computer, encrypted with Windows DPAPI. No data is sent to any server other than Google's own APIs.

Sharing

The app does not sell, share or transfer any user data to third parties. Use of data received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Removing access

You can disconnect the account in the app at any time, or revoke access at https://myaccount.google.com/permissions.
